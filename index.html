Privacy

Your notes never leave your phone

Elite Memo Pro is built so that it cannot send your data anywhere. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so Android itself prevents it from opening a network socket.

  • No account, no sign-in, no server component.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Notes and labels stored in an on-device Room database.
  • Optional encrypted-at-rest protection via your device.

About the app

Elite Memo Pro, in plain words

Text notes and freehand drawings in one place — free, offline, and entirely yours.

Write it, or draw it

Write notes with titles and checklist items, or sketch on a drawing canvas with pen, marker, highlighter, selection and eraser tools. Choose a canvas background — blank, ruled, grid or dotted — and undo or redo changes as you go.

Keep everything organised

Labels, colours and pins keep your notes in order. Search titles, text and tags with colourful match highlighting, or filter by label and colour. Switch between grid and list layouts, and sort by last modified, created date, title or colour.

Archive, trash and reminders

Archive notes to set them aside, or move them to Trash where they can be restored or permanently deleted — trashed notes are cleared automatically after 30 days. Set a reminder on a note and get a notification at the scheduled time.

Lock it, pin it, back it up

An optional app lock asks for biometric authentication or your device credential when the app opens. Pin a checklist to your home screen with a widget, share a note as plain text, and back up or restore every note to a JSON file in Downloads.

Everything stays on your device

Notes and labels are stored in the app's local Room database. There is no account, no sync service and no INTERNET permission, so nothing can leave your phone. Light, dark or system appearance — your choice.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Elite Memo Pro is free and open source under the MIT License. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no premium tier.

Does it send my notes anywhere?

No. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so Android blocks it from opening a network connection. There is no account, no server and no sync service.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (API 24) and newer. The universal APK covers all architectures, and separate per-ABI builds are published for smaller downloads.

How do I back up my notes?

Back up and restore all notes to a JSON file in your Downloads folder, with optional automatic backups. Everything stays on your device — there is no cloud copy.

Where do I get updates?

Every release is published on the GitHub releases page. The app is also packaged for F-Droid-compatible repositories, so you can update through a client.

Can I build it myself?

Absolutely — that is the point of an open licence. Clone the repository, open it with JDK 21 and the Android SDK, and run ./gradlew assembleRelease.
